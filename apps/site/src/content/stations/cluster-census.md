> **Word bank + sanity checks**
> **Word bank:**
> - **Initial mass function (IMF):** the distribution of masses stars are born with. A
>   property of a *population*, not of any one star.
> - **High-mass slope $\alpha$:** how steeply the number of stars falls with mass. The
>   canonical value is $2.3$. Steeper means fewer massive stars.
> - **Main-sequence turnoff:** the heaviest star still shining. It falls as a cluster ages,
>   which is how real clusters are dated.
> - **Remnant:** what a star leaves when its main-sequence lifetime runs out — a white
>   dwarf, neutron star or black hole, set by its initial mass.
>
> **Key relationship:**
>
> $$\xi(m) \propto m^{-\alpha}$$
>
> **Sanity checks:**
> - Around 80 percent of the stars sit below $1\,M_\odot$. If your histogram does not fall
>   steeply to the right, check which law is selected.
> - The three tally numbers must add up to the number of stars you asked for.
> - Raising the number of stars does not change the *shape* of the histogram, only how
>   closely the bars follow the smooth curve.
> - The turnoff can only fall as age rises. If it goes up, you changed something else.
> - The bars and the curve are computed separately. They should agree closely at the
>   left-hand end and disagree visibly in the last few bins, where there are few stars.
